<p><b>Vivian is popular. Hazel is new, and shy. Can they be friends?</b></p>
<p>Vivian is excited to start school again — and even more thrilled to help and make friends with new girl Hazel. But do Vivian’s friends approve of Hazel, or even let her be Hazel’s friend? <p>So begins <i>The Shy Girl &amp; The Popular Girl</i>, a school story by young author Anshika Mahesh. Told in both girls’ voices, it is about kindness, courage, and what true friendship looks like.</p>
