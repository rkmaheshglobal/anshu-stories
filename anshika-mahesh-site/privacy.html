Privacy

We count stories, not people

This is a parent-managed site for a young author. There are no accounts, no public comments, and no advertising cookies.

We use GoatCounter to see which pages are opened. It stores a page path, a rough location, the screen size, and the referring site. It does not store names, emails, or a profile of a child.

When someone reads a book, we also record three anonymous events: the story was opened, the reader reached about halfway, and the reader reached the last page. That helps Anshika know which tales people finish.

Local previews on your own computer are not counted. Browsers that send a Do Not Track signal are respected by GoatCounter.

If you join Anshika’s Story Club, your name and email are forwarded to the parent inbox at rkmaheshglobal@gmail.com so we can send monthly club notes. Reply to those emails to stop them. We do not sell this list.

Questions: use the parent inbox on Connect.
