About

Hi, I’m Anshika.

I’m 12 years old, and I write stories about friendship, courage, secrets, and the quiet moments that change everything.

I started writing in notebooks — covers first, characters second, then chapter after chapter until the story felt finished. My readers began as friends. Now this site is where those stories can travel a little farther.

I love dual points of view, sidekicks with terrible time sense, and endings where people choose kindness even when it isn’t easy.

When I’m not writing, I’m usually thinking about the next plot twist… or drawing what a nameless sidekick might look like.
