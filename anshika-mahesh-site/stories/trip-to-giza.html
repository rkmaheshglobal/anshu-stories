By Anshika Mahesh

Chapter One

The Big Project

Samyuktha and Anshika were working on a big project about the Great Pyramid of Giza for a school competition. They wanted to make it unique, so they found some cardboard in a drawer and built an amazing model.

When they finished, it looked exactly like the real pyramid!

But there was one big problem — they didn’t know much about the history of the pyramids. What were they going to do?

Chapter Two

A Magic Journey

Suddenly, they touched their model, and WHOOSH! They dived right into ancient Egypt!

When they opened their eyes, hot sand was under their feet, and the sun beat down warmly. They were so surprised that they started pinching themselves to make sure they weren’t dreaming.

Chapter Three

Welcome to Giza

A friendly man saw their confused faces and smiled. “Welcome to the Great Pyramid of Giza! Would you like a tour?”

“Yes, thank you!” they replied excitedly.

Chapter Four

Riding the Ships of the Desert

To their delight, the man brought two camels. “It’s not far, but for the full experience, would you like a ride?”

“Yes, we would love to try!” they shouted.

He helped them climb onto the tall, swaying camels. As they rode through the golden desert, the camels’ hooves made soft thud-thud sounds in the sand. Finally, they reached the massive pyramid shining under the blue sky.

Chapter Five

Secrets of the Tomb

“What is this pyramid used for?” asked Samyuktha.

“It is a grand tomb for powerful Egyptian pharaohs,” replied the man. “They preserved the bodies as mummies so people would never forget them.”

“Why do they use salt for mummifying?” asked Anshika.

“Ancient Egyptians used a special salt called natron to dry the body and stop it from rotting. That way, it lasted forever inside the pyramid!”

Chapter Six

Ancient Engineering

“Are only Egyptian pharaohs buried here?” asked Anshika.

“Mostly Egyptian kings and queens,” the guide explained. “But history has stories of others honored nearby. For example, Alexander the Great from Macedonia had a famous tomb not far away.”

“Was any machine used to build this pyramid?” asked Samyuktha, looking up at the giant stones.

“No machines like today! They used inclined planes — or ramps — to lift huge stones from the ground to higher levels.”

Chapter Seven

Home Again

Suddenly, the desert began to fade.

“We need to go now, but thank you so much!” cried Anshika.

“I never knew a pyramid could have so much meaning,” added Samyuktha.

“You are very welcome,” the man replied with a wave.

POOF! They were back home, blinking in surprise.

Chapter Eight

The Winners

The next day at school, their teacher loved the project and all the fascinating facts they shared.

Samyuktha and Anshika won First Place in the competition!

They were thrilled to have visited the Great Pyramid of Giza — even if it was just a magical dream.

The End
